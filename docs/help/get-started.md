From an empty workspace to a first answer from what you wrote, in about ten minutes. The Inbox keeps these five steps until they are done.

## The five steps

<!-- include setup-steps -->

1. **Say what the business is about**. Paste a paragraph or write a few lines. Every agent reads it first.
2. **Write your first context item**. Two sentences and a source. You own the domain Company, so it goes live at once.
3. **Ask a question about it**. See the answer an agent gets, from what you just wrote.
4. **Connect your agent**. Paste one prompt into Claude, Cursor or another agent, and approve it once.
5. **Connect your definitions**. Connect where your metrics are defined, so their names arrive by themselves. It can wait.

<!-- /include -->

## Your first answer

Ask about what you wrote. The answer starts with an answer line, such as Checked, and cites your context item. If it says Not found, nothing applies to that question yet: that is the honest answer, not an error.

> **It can wait.** Connecting your definitions is the last step on purpose. Context works without it; metric names just arrive by themselves once it is done.
