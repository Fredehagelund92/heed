## Before you start

- You'll: write one fact and send it to the owner of its domain to review.
- Who: anyone who can sign in.
- You need: the fact, a link to where you read it, and about five minutes.

The example on this page is the stop on online sales to Norway from the demo data: online sales to Norway stopped on 4 August 2026.

## Steps

1. Choose **Add context** in the header, or press C.

   The form opens with an empty draft. It's saved as you type.

2. Pick the kind. For something that happened on a date, keep **Event**.

   The other kinds are Note, Guide, Example and Issue. The [glossary](glossary.md#facts) says what each one is for.

3. Pick the **Domain**: Sales.

   **Who reviews it** then names the owner who will check your fact.

4. Write the **Title**: _Online sales to Norway stopped on 4 August 2026_.

5. Write the **Summary** in one or two sentences. AI agents read it first, so say what happened and when.

6. Under **Where it applies**, find and add Orders and Norway.

   Agents get the fact when a question is about these. To cover every market or every product, pick **Any value**.

7. Under **When it applies**, set **From** to 4 Aug 2026. Leave **Until** empty while it's still going on.

   The last day is included.

8. Under **Where this comes from**, add the **Link to the source**, **A sentence copied from the source** and the **Date you read it**.

   The owner opens the link to check the fact. We keep the link and the sentence, never the whole document.

9. Under **What not to conclude**, say what someone might wrongly conclude from the fact. You can leave it empty.

   Tap a sentence starter, such as _It doesn't tell you ..._, or **Use this** on the example. The card under the box shows what an agent gets. Agents get this line with the fact and are told not to draw that conclusion.

10. Open the **What agents get** tab to see what an agent will get once the fact is accepted.

11. Choose **Send for review**.

Tip: the assistant can draft a fact for you from a paragraph you paste. Check every field before you send it.

## What happens next

- Your proposal shows under **Proposals**, in **Mine**, as **Waiting for review**.
- The owner of the domain reviews it. They accept it, ask for changes or reject it, and you're told which.
- Once it's accepted, agents get your fact at once.

## If something goes wrong

- _It isn't sent when you choose **Send for review**._ A field marked **Needed** is empty, or a check found a problem. Each one is named. Fix them, then send it again.
- _You're not sure of the domain._ Pick the closest one. If the fact belongs to another domain, the owner asks for changes and says which.
- _You're not sure it's true._ Don't send it yet. Ask the person who knows, or add what you know to the summary and say what isn't confirmed.
