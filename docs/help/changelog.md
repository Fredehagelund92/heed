## Filter by any dimension: pick it, then its values

2026-11-10

Overview's Filter button, or F, opens one list: pick a field or a dimension, then its values, the ones agents asked about most first, with what each counts, a search, A to Z, and is or is not. Type two letters to find a value in any dimension.

A filter reads in parts, such as Store · is any of · 3 values, and a click on its middle flips is and is not. Old links that filter by a market still open.

## Select days on the chart, and drill into a bar

2026-11-03

Drag across Overview's chart, or press B and then Shift and the arrows, to select some days: the numbers add up just those days, the filter bar says which, and a link keeps them. Press a part of a bar to look at only that answer. Escape clears the days.

## Filters as chips, and facets with counts

2026-11-03

Overview's filters are chips you add from the bar (press F, or / to type), change in place, move and take off one at a time. A filter can keep a value or leave it out, and you can filter by market and metric too. Facets beside the topics show every value with what it counts. A link keeps every filter, and Back undoes the last change.

## Overview and Questions are one page

2026-11-03

Overview now holds the topics too: the numbers, questions per day and the topics follow the same range, domain, kind of agent, view and filters, and a changed number counts to its new value. Questions' links open Overview at the topics.

## "What is" starts with the definition

2026-11-03

Ask what a metric is, and the answer starts with its definition, in the words of the definitions source. With no context written about it, a Definition mark stands where the answer line would be.

## The review's Preview is the item's page

2026-11-03

In the review, Preview shows the item as its Context page will read once you publish it.

## Emoji in comments

2026-11-03

Type : in a comment to open the emoji list, and letters to narrow it.

## The Inbox pane starts with what agents say

2026-11-03

Select a card in the Inbox to see what agents say today, and what they will say after you decide. The button at the foot is named for the decision, and the other choices are buttons beside it, with their names.

## Observability opens on Overview

2026-11-02

Observability no longer has a Summary page or its monthly digests. It opens on Overview, and an old link to Summary or a digest lands there.

## Ask reads like a chat

2026-11-02

The answer line is a badge at the head of the answer, and the badge opens a card that says what it means. Sources are chips under the answer, and its actions are one row of icons. A question that gets nothing back stays in the chat, with Ask again.

## Conflicts are a filter of Context

2026-11-02

Conflicts is a filter of the one Context list, and every item in a conflict has a Conflict chip. A link to the old Conflicts list opens the filtered list.

## Ask writes its answer as it goes

2026-11-01

An answer in Ask arrives a sentence at a time, and Esc stops it. Ask takes one question at a time. Ask next is gone: Ask offers questions only after one it could not answer.

## Ask about Definitions

2026-11-01

Ask answers questions about what Definitions lists, such as "list 10 dimensions", "how many metrics" or "what markets are there". When it looks nothing up, it says what it can answer.

## Publish without review

2026-10-31

An owner can let one agent publish low-risk new items in their domain without a review round, once an admin switches it on. Each one names the owner, and can be undone from its history.

## Summary shows what was caught

2026-10-31

Summary lists what was caught on three lines of their own: answers that went out with a warning, saved questions whose answer changed, and alerts with how many an owner acted on. The lines are never added up.

## Compare two items that disagree

2026-10-31

Two context items that disagree open side by side. Keep this one, Keep both, Not a conflict or Ask the owner, each with why. Ask, the Inbox and the review lead to the same view with Compare and decide.

## Every agent has a name

2026-10-31

Every connected agent is listed by its name, with who it works for. An admin switches each of its tools on or off in its Details.

## A monthly digest of Summary

2026-10-30

Each finished month keeps a copy of Summary as it was. Open one from Summary to read it or print it on one page.

## Comment on a line in the review

2026-10-30

In the review, press C on a line or a field to leave a comment there. A comment can be resolved, and opened again.

## The review shows the source

2026-10-30

The review opens on Source: each field before and after, and the text with line numbers and the changed words marked. Preview shows the rich text.

## Ask about the context itself

2026-10-30

Ask answers questions about the context's own records: what was created or changed, what is due for a check, what is archived, who owns a domain, how many context items there are, and what agents asked that found nothing.

## Every list sorts both ways

2026-10-30

Choose a sort a second time to turn it around: A to Z becomes Z to A, and Newest first becomes Oldest first.

## Try a figure

2026-10-30

Figures show as the second connection of your definitions source, and Try a figure reads one value to show that the connection works.

## Access keys

2026-10-29

An Admin can make an access key for a bot or a scheduled job in Settings. A key names no person.

## Connect a tool in five steps

2026-10-29

Settings, Integrations connects a tool in five steps, from picking it to its schedule. Change settings goes through the same steps, and Disconnect stops reading it.

## Saved questions and alerts

2026-10-28

A saved question is checked again, and Quality shows whether its answer is the same, changed or needs a look. An alert rule raises a card in the owner's Inbox.

## Documents become suggestions

2026-10-28

Connect Markdown files in Git, or Google Drive, as a content source, and its documents arrive as suggestions to review.

## Related items

2026-10-28

A context page lists the items related to it, and opens one beside the page.

## Sign in with your company's account

2026-10-27

People can sign in with the company's OpenID Connect provider. An Admin invites people and gives them the Admin or Member role.

## Help in one place

2026-10-23

One Help menu in the sidebar, a short Help page, a card on every answer line, and ? for the keys.

## Agents can follow links

2026-10-23

An agent can follow a link between context items, definitions and domains, one step at a time. Related shows the same.

## Synonyms are tags

2026-10-22

Type other names for a definition and a comma; a word that already means something else says who has it.

## Event, Warning, Rule and Guide

2026-10-22

The four kinds of context have short, plain names.

## The review waits while you type

2026-10-21

Single keys never act in a note, offline publishing waits, and Publish the ones you've read lists them first.

## Connect an agent with one prompt

2026-10-20

A live line shows waiting, then Connected, with the agent's name and yours.
