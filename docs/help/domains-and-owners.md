Who publishes what, how to add a domain, the sandbox, and inviting people.

## Domains

<!-- include word: Domain -->

An area of the business, such as Sales or Marketing. Every context item belongs to one.

<!-- /include -->

## Owners

<!-- include word: Owner -->

The person who publishes and checks a domain's context. Agents read only what an owner has published.

<!-- /include -->

- Admins do everything, including roles and invitations.
- An owner who isn't an admin edits their own domain and adds owners to it.
- A domain with no context can be deleted; the sandbox never reaches agents.

## People

Everyone is an Admin or a Member. Owning a domain isn't a role: it's set on the domain, and shows on both the domain's page and People.

- **Invite people** in Settings, People: their addresses, Admin or Member, and the domains they own from the start. An owner who isn't an admin invites from their own domain's page.
- Each invite gives a link to copy and send them. It works for 7 days. Resend makes a new link, and the old one stops working.
- Removing someone waits until no domain has them as its only owner. What they published stays, with their name, and Undo brings them back.

## Signing in

People sign in with their company account. An invite is accepted the first time they sign in with the address it was sent to. A session ends after 12 hours unused, or 7 days after signing in.
