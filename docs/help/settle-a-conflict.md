Two context items are in a conflict when an owner said they disagree, in the review or in the editor. The app never decides it by itself. Until someone settles it, agents give both and say that context disagrees.

## Compare

Compare and decide shows the two side by side: what each says, where it comes from (its domain, owners, check date, source and the line copied from it), and what agents would say with only that one.

- When the two are written apart, the words both share are grey, so the words where they part stand out.
- When one is a near copy of the other, the words that differ are underlined.
- When neither helps, one line says so. Read both and the check dates.

Conflicts in Context lists every pair, most asked first, with yours first under Mine.

## Settle it

Each way asks why first, and can take a line from a source that settles it. Before you confirm, you see what agents will then say.

- **Keep this one** archives the other. It needs an owner of the other item, or an admin.
- **Edit this one** opens the editor with the other item beside it, to narrow it to where it applies.
- **Keep both** records why. Agents keep saying Conflict, and the pair leaves the conflict lists until either item changes.
- **Not a conflict** removes the flag. It needs an owner of the item that holds it, or an admin. Agents then give both and say nothing about a difference.
- **Ask the owner** mentions them in a comment on the item, and it reaches their Inbox.

A button you can't use says why. If you own only one of the two, you can suggest archiving the other, which goes to its owners with your reason. The other owners are told of every decision, it is on both items' history, and you can undo it for a few seconds.

## Keys

In the conflict view, Left and Right pick the item, 1 and 2 keep the first or the second, E edits, B keeps both, N is not a conflict, O asks the owner, and J and K move through the list. Command and Enter confirms the reason, and Esc closes it and keeps what you wrote. No key acts while you type.
