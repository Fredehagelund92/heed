A suggestion waits in its owner's Inbox. The quick view says what changes; the review is where you decide.

## The quick view

Select a suggestion in the Inbox to see what changes, who sent it, and anything that needs care, such as claims to check. Review opens the full screen.

## The review

- The change, one block per kind of change: wording, formatting, a link, the order, dates.
- Try it: what agents will say with the draft, and what they say now.
- The Ready line, which never says more than was checked: new numbers in an edit say they aren't checked against the source.
- Publish, or Return with a note: the note is the first comment, and the sender answers there.
- A suggestion to archive that its sender took back in the comments shows their words. Close the suggestion and nothing changes, or archive anyway, which asks first.

## Taking back a suggestion to archive

Suggested archiving a context item, and changed your mind? Say why in its comments, in your Inbox under All, then choose Withdraw the suggestion under your comment. The owner sees your words in the review and closes it, or archives anyway. Keep the suggestion under the same comment lets it stand again.

## Publishing several

Publish the ones you've read takes only suggestions you read: the whole change on screen for a few seconds, or Mark as read. It lists them first, and Undo puts them all back.

> **Keys.** J and K move through the queue, N and P through the changes, Command and Enter publishes, and ? lists them all.
