A suggestion waits in its owner's Inbox. The quick view says what changes; the review is where you decide.

## The quick view

Select a suggestion in the Inbox to see, side by side, what agents say today and what they will say after you publish, who sent it, and anything that needs care, such as claims to check. A suggestion that clashes with live context shows what agents would hear if you publish it as it is, and a suggestion to archive shows what they will say after you archive. What changes and the sender's note are folded below; open them when you want them. The button at the foot opens the full screen and says what you decide there: Review, Decide how they relate for a suggestion that clashes with live context, or Check the draft for a draft of what agents could not answer. The words beside it say what follows. The other buttons say what they do, such as Edit or Archive.

## The review

- The change, as agents will read it: the fields that change as a table, then the text with what was taken out and what was added. N and P step through the changes.
- Preview: the item as its page will read once you publish it. The review keeps to the source or the preview as you go through the queue.
- Try it: what agents will say with the draft, and what they say now.
- The Ready line, which never says more than was checked: new numbers in an edit read Ready, not checked against a source.
- Comments on a line or a field: point at a line of the text or a row of the fields and choose the + beside it, or press C, or tap it on a phone. The thread hangs there. Type @ to mention someone, or : for an emoji. Anyone who reads the suggestion can resolve it, or reopen it, and the thread says who did and when. The side panel counts the open ones and goes to each. Open threads never hold Publish.
- Publish, or Return with a note: the note is the first comment, and the sender answers there.
- A suggestion to archive that its sender took back in the comments shows their words. Close the suggestion and nothing changes, or archive anyway, which asks first.

## Taking back a suggestion to archive

Suggested archiving a context item, and changed your mind? Say why in its comments, in your Inbox under All, then choose Withdraw the suggestion under your comment. The owner sees your words in the review and closes it, or archives anyway. Keep the suggestion under the same comment lets it stand again.

## Publishing several

Publish the ones you've read takes only suggestions you read: the whole change on screen for a few seconds, or Mark as read. It lists them first, and Undo puts them all back.

> **Keys.** J and K move through the queue, N and P through the changes, the arrows through the lines, C comments on the one in focus, Command and Enter publishes, Esc goes back to the Inbox, and ? lists them all.
