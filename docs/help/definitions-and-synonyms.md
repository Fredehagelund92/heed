Metrics and dimensions, the business's own terms, and other names for them.

## Metrics and dimensions

They come from where your metrics are defined, and are read-only here: their name, meaning and other names belong to that source. Write context about them instead.

## Terms and synonyms

<!-- include word: Synonym -->

Another name for a metric or a dimension, such as GMV for Gross sales. Agents read it as that definition.

<!-- /include -->

- A term is a word with its own meaning, such as Peak season.
- On a definition, type other names into Also called: a comma or Enter makes each one a tag.
- In a term's editor, Another name for points the word at a metric or a dimension.
- A word that already means something else says who has it, and is never saved twice.
